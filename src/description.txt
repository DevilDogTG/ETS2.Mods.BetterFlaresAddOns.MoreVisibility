[orange]DriveDogs: Better Flares More Visibility (DOT 7500K)

[normal]An add-on for Better Flares v4.8 - Headlights DOT 7500k. Your truck's headlights reach
farther and spread wider for more comfortable night driving, and the road stays readable in bad
daytime conditions (rain, fog, overcast) - while keeping Better Flares' realistic mood.
--------------------
[orange]What changes
[normal]- Brighter beams: every truck headlight stage (low, high, front and roof lamps) is 35%
  brighter than Better Flares v4.8's own DOT 7500k values.
- Longer reach: low beam reaches 30% farther, high beam and front lamps 40%, roof lamps 50%.
- Wider beams: a few degrees wider, so road shoulders and curves are better lit.
- Better near-field light: the area just in front of the bumper is lit more evenly.
- More visible headlamps: the glow of the lamp itself is a little larger and stays visible from
  wider angles, so trucks read better in poor daylight.

What does NOT change:
- Beam aim, beam shape and cut-off (Better Flares' v4.8 pattern is kept as-is).
- Light color temperature (still DOT 7500k).
- Tail lights, indicators, side markers and all other lights.
--------------------
[orange]Requirements / load order
[normal]Top = highest priority in Mod Manager:
1. DriveDogs: Better Flares More Visibility (DOT 7500K)   <- this mod
2. Better Flares v4.8 - Headlights DOT 7500k
3. Better Flares v4.8 - Base

Only for Better Flares v4.8 with the DOT 7500k headlight option. It replaces the truck headlight
definitions, so it is not compatible with other Better Flares headlight options or with any other
mod that replaces truck head_light files. The headlamp glow change may also apply to other
vehicles that use Better Flares' headlight glow, such as AI traffic.
--------------------

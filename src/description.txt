[orange]DriveDogs: Better Flares More Visibility (7500K LED)

[normal]An add-on for Better Flares v4.9 - Headlights.2 7500K LED. Your truck's headlights spread
wider and the high beam reaches farther for more comfortable night driving, and the headlamp glow
stays readable in bad daytime conditions (rain, fog, overcast) - while keeping Better Flares'
realistic mood.
--------------------
[orange]What changes
[normal]- Wider beams: every truck headlight stage (low, high, front and roof lamps) spreads 103
  degrees instead of 90, so road shoulders and curves are better lit.
- Longer high beam: high beam and front lamps reach 280 instead of 240 / 260.
- More visible headlamps: the glow of the lamp itself is a little larger and stays visible from
  wider angles, so trucks read better in poor daylight.

What does NOT change:
- Brightness, low beam and roof lamp reach, and the light just ahead of the bumper: Better Flares
  v4.9's own values are kept.
- Beam aim and beam pattern: your Better Flares Headlights.1 choice (any of them) is kept.
- Light color temperature (7500K LED).
- Tail lights, indicators, side markers and all other lights.
--------------------
[orange]Requirements / load order
[normal]Top = highest priority in Mod Manager:
1. DriveDogs: Better Flares More Visibility (7500K LED)   <- this mod
2. Better Flares - Headlights.2 7500K LED
3. Better Flares - Headlights.1 (any one)
4. Better Flares - City Lights Addon (optional)
5. Better Flares - Traffic Addon (optional)
6. Better Flares - Base

Only for Better Flares v4.9. Headlights.1 and Headlights.2 are both required. This mod replaces
Headlights.2's beam values, so loaded above another Headlights.2 colour it still gives 7500K
light. The headlamp glow change may also apply to other vehicles that use Better Flares'
headlight glow, such as AI traffic.
--------------------
